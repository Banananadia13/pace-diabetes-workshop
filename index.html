Do ketones open GLUT4 doors?

No. Ketones are not glucose and do not use GLUT4. They enter muscle through monocarboxylate transporters (mainly MCT1), moving down their concentration gradient without needing insulin. Strong

Inside the cell, mitochondria convert them (via the enzymes BDH1 and SCOT) to acetyl-CoA, which is burned for energy. Muscle, heart and brain all burn ketones; the liver makes them but cannot use them. Strong

In ketoacidosis, the liver makes ketones faster than muscle and other tissues can burn them, so they build up in the blood and it becomes acidic. Strong

High ketones may even make muscle less responsive to insulin: in isolated mouse muscle, 5 mM β-hydroxybutyrate blocked part of the insulin signal (Akt) and cut insulin-stimulated glucose uptake in oxidative muscle. Human relevance is still being studied. Emerging

Sources: Puchalska & Crawford, Cell Metab 2017; Arima, J Atheroscler Thromb 2023; Am J Physiol Endocrinol Metab 2010 (β-hydroxybutyrate and muscle glucose transport); GPnotebook, DKA pathogenesis.

Insulin rises first, glucose follows

Illustrative. Insulin resistance makes the pancreas release more insulin for years, keeping glucose near normal. Glucose only rises once the beta cells can no longer keep up.

Contraction moves GLUT4 to the surface through AMPK and other pathways, separate from the insulin receptor. Uptake and insulin sensitivity stay higher for up to about 48 hours (Pereira 2017). Illustrative

What the research says

Plaque starts when LDL particles slip through a damaged lining and are trapped in the artery wall. Modified LDL is swallowed by macrophages through scavenger receptors, which turns them into foam cells. Strong

In diabetes, glucose and reactive by-products such as methylglyoxal attach to LDL (glycation). Glycated LDL is recognised less by normal LDL receptors and more by macrophage receptors, which drives cholesterol loading in cell studies (Brown 2005). Only a few per cent of LDL is glycated in people, so it adds to the effect of LDL level, blood pressure and smoking rather than replacing it. Moderate

Advanced glycation end products cross-link collagen and damage elastin, stiffening arteries. Stiffer arteries raise pulse wave velocity, systolic pressure and pulse pressure (Li 2025). Moderate

Exercise training improves lining function in type 2 diabetes: flow-mediated dilation up 1.8% overall and 2.5% with combined aerobic and resistance training (Qiu 2018, 16 studies). Strong

Ketoacidosis warning signs

Nausea or vomiting, stomach pain, deep or laboured breathing, fruity breath, dehydration or confusion need urgent medical care. With an SGLT2 inhibitor this can happen even when glucose is close to normal.
